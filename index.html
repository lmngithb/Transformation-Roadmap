Part 2 · Who this is for

Our reader is the Chief Digital Officer of a mid-sized container carrier or global freight forwarder, with roughly US$2–5 billion in revenue, whose board has asked two questions this quarter: should we join an industry data-sharing platform, and what does our three-year digital plan look like? They already run some digital customer tools but have never sequenced them against the data foundations underneath, and they do not yet know who inside the company can say “stop” when a platform is not working.

Part 3 · Benefits

What that reader can do with it.

Decide what to defer if industry standards slip

The roadmap ties TradeLens (R3) to the data-standards work in C2 (arrow D4) and the customer-tool adoption in W2 (arrow D7). A reader can see that an ecosystem launch should wait until both are in place.

Roadmap: R3

Name who can stop an ecosystem platform

Decision 6 makes one role accountable for continue, realign or decommission calls on a platform like TradeLens, and lists who must be consulted first: the CEO, CTIO, IBM and General Counsel.
