## What this is

A three-part deliverable for Maersk's digital transformation (2015–2023): a Crawl / Walk / Run roadmap, a governance RACI, and a quarterly dashboard. Together they answer one question for an executive reader: **who decides, and when.**

## The three deliverables

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Roadmap](https://lmngithb.github.io/Transformation-Roadmap/roadmap.html) | Eight initiatives sequenced from an inventory of 16, on an editable timeline, with seven named dependencies and a rationale for each phase. |
| [RACI](https://lmngithb.github.io/Transformation-Roadmap/raci.html) | Seven recurring decisions, each with one Accountable role. People can be dragged between R, A, C and I. |
| [Dashboard](https://lmngithb.github.io/Transformation-Roadmap/dashboard.html) | One screen showing phases, health and decisions, one quarter at a time, with eight metrics tied to initiatives. |

The [home page](https://lmngithb.github.io/Transformation-Roadmap/index.html) covers the team, the intended reader, the benefits, and what we added beyond the brief.

## Who it's for

The Chief Digital Officer of a mid-sized container carrier or global freight forwarder (roughly US$2-5 billion in revenue). Their board has asked two questions: should we join an industry data-sharing platform, and what does our three-year digital plan look like?

## Key finding

We sequenced by dependency. TradeLens sits in **Run**, not Crawl, because its prerequisite (industry data standards adopted by other carriers) was outside Maersk's control. Of the 16 inventoried initiatives we sequenced eight, and left out autonomous-vessel AI and VR training because neither had a dependency we could name.
